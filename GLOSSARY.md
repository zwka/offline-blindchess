# Offline Blind Chess

This context covers blindfold chess training drills, the practice history they produce, and installing the training app on a mobile device.

## Language

**Square Colors**:
A training drill where a player identifies a chess square's light or dark color from its coordinate.

**Same Color**:
A training drill where a player decides whether two chess squares have the same color.

**Diagonals**:
A training drill where a player decides whether two chess squares lie on the same diagonal.

**Knight**:
A training drill where a player decides whether a knight can legally move from one square to another in a single move.

**Quadrant**:
A training drill where a player identifies which of the board's four quadrants contains a given square.

**Coordinates**:
A training drill where a player locates the chessboard square represented by a coordinate.

**Attempt**:
One completed 60-second round of a training drill.

**Training Module**:
One of the available chess training drills, such as Square Colors, Same Color, Coordinates, Diagonals, Quadrant, or Knight.

**Score**:
The number of correct answers in one attempt.

**Accuracy**:
The percentage of answered prompts in an attempt that were correct.

**Attempt History**:
A chronological record of a player's completed attempts for one training module.

**Personal Best**:
The highest score a player has achieved in one training module.

**Mobile Installation**:
The process of adding the training app to a phone's home screen from a mobile browser.
